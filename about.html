About Ker0n3 Media

Ker0n3 Media is a skin and a companion add-on for Kodi, for TV and touch devices. It is a personal project shared with family and friends, distributed through this repository under the GPL-3.0 licence.

Trakt

Ker0n3 Media uses Trakt but is not endorsed or certified by Trakt.

Linking a Trakt account is optional. When a user links it, Ker0n3 Media syncs that user's own watched history, playback progress and watchlist between their devices. The same notice is shown inside the app, under Settings → Accounts → Trakt.

Other services

Titles, artwork and episode data come from TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Users may alternatively sync with Simkl.
