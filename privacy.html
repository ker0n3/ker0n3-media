Ker0n3 Media privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026.

Ker0n3 Media is a skin and a companion add-on for Kodi. It runs on the user's own device. The project does not operate a server that stores users' accounts, tokens or viewing history.

What is stored, and where

  • Watched history and playback progress: kept in a local database in Kodi's data folder on the device, so the app can show what to continue watching.
  • Account authorisations: if the user links Trakt, Simkl or a debrid account, the access token for that service is stored only on the device, in Kodi's data folder. Passwords are never asked for or stored: linking is done with a code on the service's own website.
  • Cached catalogue data: titles, artwork and lists are cached on the device to make the app faster.

What is sent, and to whom

  • Trakt (only if linked): the user's own watched items, playback progress and watchlist changes are sent to and read from that user's Trakt account, to keep their devices in sync. Nothing is read from or written to any other account.
  • Simkl (only if linked): the same, with that user's Simkl account.
  • TMDB and the other services the app queries: the identifiers of the titles being browsed or played, as needed to show information and find streams.
  • Anonymous usage count: once a day the skin sends a random device identifier, the skin variant (TV or touch) and its version to a counter run by the author, to know how many devices use each version. It contains no account data and no viewing data, and the IP address is not stored.

No data is sold or shared with third parties for advertising, and no viewing history leaves the device except to the tracking service the user chose to link.

How long data is kept

Local data stays on the device until the user removes it. The author keeps no copy of users' history or tokens.

Disconnecting and deleting

  • In the app: Settings → Accounts → choose the service → Unlink. This deletes the stored token from the device and the app stops accessing that account.
  • The user can also revoke the app at any time from their Trakt account settings (Connected apps) or the equivalent page of the other service.
  • Uninstalling the add-on and clearing Kodi's data folder removes the local database and all cached data.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/ker0n3/ker0n3-media.
